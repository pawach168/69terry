My Journey – The Story of Pawach

From Electronics Engineering to Technology, Innovation and Business Development

1. Introduction

Hello! My name is Pawach Metharattanarak. I am an engineer by education and a business development professional by experience. I have a strong interest in technology, innovation, entrepreneurship, and solving real-world problems through practical solutions.

My professional journey combines engineering knowledge, sales, marketing, product development, and business strategy. I enjoy learning new technologies and connecting technical ideas with real business opportunities.

2. Education and Engineering Background

I graduated with a Bachelor of Engineering in Electronics Engineering from King Mongkut's Institute of Technology Ladkrabang (KMITL). I studied there from 2000 to 2004.

During university, I developed an interest in electronics, semiconductor technology, and scientific research. My senior project involved CIGS thin-film solar cells and the development of a CdS window layer using the Chemical Bath Deposition (CBD) process.

My junior project involved a 5-micron CMOS process in collaboration with NECTEC. These experiences helped me understand the relationship between engineering principles, research, and practical technology applications.

3. Professional Experience

Throughout my career, I have gained experience in technology sales, business development, marketing, product management, and strategic partnerships. I have worked with various technology products and solutions, including:

  • Security Systems: CCTV, IP Cameras, Access Control, and integrated security.
  • Renewable Energy: Solar PV systems, solar solutions, and energy management.
  • Power Solutions: UPS, Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS), and backup power.
  • Smart Technology: IoT, Smart Community, Smart Agriculture, and environmental monitoring.
  • Software and Digital Solutions: AI, cloud applications, and digital transformation.

I have worked with organizations such as Panasonic, AMR, BACOM, PTA Design, VST ECS, and other technology-related businesses. These experiences have helped me understand different industries, customer requirements, distribution channels, and project-based businesses.

4. My Current Business Direction

Currently, I am involved with TA Solution / IP Integration in strategic sales, marketing, and business development. My responsibilities include identifying business opportunities, developing partnerships, coordinating with technology providers, and connecting customers with suitable solutions.

My interests cover several business areas:

  • Smart Security and CCTV Integration

I believe that successful innovation is not just about creating new technology. It is about understanding people's needs and turning ideas into sustainable business opportunities.

6. Personal Interests and Entrepreneurship
